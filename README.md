## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a final-year IT Engineering student at EMSI Rabat specializing in Digital Development and Information Systems. My core focus is on designing resilient backend architectures using Spring Boot and FastAPI, as well as integrating automated DevSecOps pipelines. I am actively seeking an End-of-Studies Internship (PFE) starting in February 2027.

* 🎓 **Education:** I am currently in my final year of the IT and Networks Engineering program at EMSI Rabat. Previously, I earned a Specialized Technician Diploma in Full-Stack Web Development from ISTA (Class Rank: 3rd).
* 💼 **DevOps & Security Experience:** As a DevOps/DevSecOps Intern at Capgemini Engineering, I designed a GitLab CI/CD pipeline orchestrating 9 generative AI microservices. I also integrated shift-left security controls, including Semgrep, Gitleaks, and Trivy.
* 🧠 **AI & Full-Stack Experience:** During my internship at CGI, I developed an HR Chatbot using React, FastAPI, and MySQL. This included implementing a RAG pipeline and intent classification for automated CV parsing and candidate matching.
* 🏆 **Certifications:** I hold certifications as an Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) DevOps Professional, OCI Architect Professional, and Java SE 17 Developer.
* 📫 **Contact:** You can reach me at [salah.khadir@outlook.com](mailto:salah.khadir@outlook.com).
